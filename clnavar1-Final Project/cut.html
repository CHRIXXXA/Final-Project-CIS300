Lower-Calorie Recipes for Cutting

These recipes are lighter but still filling, with a focus on lean protein, veggies, and simple ingredients.

Grilled Chicken Salad

~350–400 calories

Ingredients

  • 4 oz grilled chicken breast
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tbsp light vinaigrette
  • Salt, pepper

Directions

  1. Add greens, tomatoes, and cucumber to a bowl.
  2. Top with sliced grilled chicken.
  3. Drizzle with vinaigrette and season with salt and pepper.

Egg White & Veggie Scramble

~250–300 calories

Ingredients

  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 whole egg (optional for extra fats)
  • 1/4 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup diced onions
  • 1/2 cup spinach
  • Salt, pepper

Directions

  1. Sauté peppers and onions in a nonstick pan.
  2. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  3. Pour in beaten egg whites (and egg if using).
  4. Scramble until cooked through; season with salt and pepper.

Greek Yogurt Parfait

~200–250 calories

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup non-fat Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup berries (strawberries, blueberries, etc.)
  • 1 tbsp granola (optional)
  • 1 tsp honey or zero-cal sweetener

Directions

  1. Add Greek yogurt to a bowl or cup.
  2. Layer with berries and granola.
  3. Drizzle with honey or sweetener if desired.
