High-Calorie Recipes for Muscle Gain

These meals are higher in calories and protein to support muscle growth while still being fairly simple to make.

Chicken & Rice Power Bowl

~650–700 calories • High protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked white or brown rice
  • 6 oz grilled chicken breast
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup black beans
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salsa, salt, pepper

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to package instructions.
  2. Season and grill chicken; slice into strips.
  3. Assemble bowl with rice, chicken, black beans, and avocado.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and top with salsa, salt, and pepper.

Overnight Protein Oats

~550–600 calories • Great breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 1 cup milk or almond milk
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1/2 banana, sliced
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)

Directions

  1. In a jar or container, mix oats, protein powder, and milk.
  2. Stir in peanut butter and honey.
  3. Top with banana slices.
  4. Refrigerate overnight and eat cold or warmed up.

Beef Pasta Meal Prep

~700–800 calories • Perfect for batch cooking

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked whole-wheat pasta
  • 6 oz lean ground beef
  • 1/2 cup marinara sauce
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1 cup spinach
  • Garlic, salt, pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta as directed on the package.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a pan with garlic, salt, and pepper.
  3. Stir in marinara sauce and spinach until wilted.
  4. Combine pasta and beef mixture; top with mozzarella.
  5. Divide into containers if meal prepping.
