Christophers Fitness Recipes

The goal of this webosite is to provide easy-to-make, macro-friendly recipes for college students looking to bulk or cut. Whether you're trying to gain muscle or shed fat, I've got you covered with delicious meals that fit your fitness goals.

About Me

Hi! I’m Christopher, a college student who loves lifting, meal prepping, and finding simple recipes that actually fit my macros. I made this website to keep all my go-to meals in one place and share them with anyone on the same journey.

What This Website Is About

You’ll find two main categories here:

  • Bulking Recipes: Higher-calorie, macro-friendly meals to help you gain muscle.
  • Cutting Recipes: Lighter meals that keep you full while you’re in a calorie deficit.
